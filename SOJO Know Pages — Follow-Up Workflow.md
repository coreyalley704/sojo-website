# The Know Pages & Your Follow-Up Workflow

**Updated:** August 26, 2026 · sojo.church

The four new Know pages walk a person from curiosity to baptism, and the last two pages end with buttons that text your Hello Church number (980-680-0958). This doc explains how each page works, exactly what arrives when someone taps a button, and the one-time setup to make the follow-up automatic.

---

## How the website connects to Hello Church

Every button is a pre-written text message. When somebody taps one, their phone's messaging app opens with the message already typed — **keyword first** — addressed to 980-680-0958. They add their name and hit send. Nothing is sent without their thumb on the button, and you receive a real text from their real number, which means you can just text back like a human. That's the whole magic.

The three keywords that can arrive:

| Keyword | The message they send | It means |
|---|---|---|
| **JESUS** | "JESUS - I just prayed to follow Jesus. My name is ___" | They prayed the prayer on Partner with Him |
| **BAPTIZE** | "BAPTIZE - I want to be baptized. My name is ___" | They're ready for the tank |
| **QUESTION** | "QUESTION - I have a question about following Jesus. My name is ___" | They're close but not there |

## The journey, page by page

**1. Yada (sojo.church/yada.html)** — *Know · Part One.* What it means to know God experientially, not just know about him. No buttons here on purpose — this page plants; it doesn't push. It hands people to page two.

**2. God's Plan for Life (gods-plan.html)** — *Part Two.* Garden → break → Abraham → Psalm 1 → Jesus and abundant life. Again no ask — story first. Hands them to page three.

**3. Partner with Him (partner.html)** — *Part Three.* Apprenticeship, grace, repent-and-believe, and a written prayer to start. **This is where the JESUS button lives** ("I prayed this — text us"), plus a QUESTION button for the not-yet-ready. Below the buttons the page tells them exactly what happens next, so texting doesn't feel like jumping off a cliff: a real person replies, then Discover More, then baptism.

**4. Baptism (baptism.html)** — *Part Four.* Who/what/why/when/where/how, honest FAQs, and the **BAPTIZE button** (with the JESUS button repeated for people who arrive here first — plenty will).

## One-time setup in Hello Church (about 15 minutes)

Open your Hello Church dashboard and create a **keyword auto-reply** for each of the three words. (If your plan calls them "keywords," "automations," or "workflows," it's the same idea: *when a text starts with X, instantly reply with Y and flag it.*) Suggested replies — steal these:

**JESUS →**
> "This is the best text we've gotten all week — welcome to the family! 🎉 A pastor (a real one, not a robot) is going to reply personally today. Two quick things while you wait: what's your first name, and did you pray that prayer today for the first time?"

**BAPTIZE →**
> "YES. So glad you texted. A pastor will reply today to set up a short, easy conversation — your story, what baptism means, any questions — and get you the very next baptism date. What's your first name?"

**QUESTION →**
> "So glad you asked — questions are how everybody gets here. A real person will text you back today. Ask us anything; nothing is off limits."

Then set a **notification** so every keyword text alerts you (and Brandy, as Outreach & Assimilation Director) immediately — these are the most time-sensitive texts your church receives.

## The human follow-up (the part that can't be automated)

- **Same day, every time.** The auto-reply buys you hours, not days. A pastor texts back personally before bedtime.
- **JESUS:** celebrate first, theology later. Get their name, invite them to Sunday, and put them on the Discover More list. Log them in Planning Center so they don't fall through the cracks.
- **BAPTIZE:** schedule the sit-down conversation within the week, then lock their date.
- **QUESTION:** answer the actual question; don't redirect to a program. People text QUESTION when they want a person.
- **Weekly rhythm:** once a week, scan the Hello Church inbox for any keyword text that never got a human reply. Zero should be the standing goal.
