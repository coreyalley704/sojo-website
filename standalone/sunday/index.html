Two things worth saying out loud

—

Twenty-five new faces

Our first Discover SOJO in the new home was packed — about twenty-five people who said, “I want to know more about this place.” So here’s your job. If you see someone you don’t recognize this morning, don’t wait for a greeter. Say hi. Learn a name. Invite them to sit with you. That’s how a crowd becomes a family.
