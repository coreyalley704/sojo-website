01 · Know life

Life

You were made to be part of something bigger than yourself.

Not a trend. Not a hack somebody invented last year. Something ancient — older than this country, older than this city — that's been practiced and handed down and tested by ordinary people in every generation since.

It has held up under war, poverty, grief, and doubt. Tried and true. Millions of people have staked their lives on it and found it holds.

That's what we mean by life. Not a better version of your schedule. A Person, and a story you belong inside.

It's older than you think. And it still works. See what a Sunday looks like →

02 · Grow in peace

Peace

The noise got so loud it killed our peace.

Notifications, outrage, comparison, the endless scroll. A world engineered to distract you from your own life. Most of us are exhausted and can't name why.

Here's what surprised us about Jesus: He isn't only interested in the big spiritual questions. He's interested in the nitty-gritty — your Tuesday, your marriage, your money, the conversation you've been avoiding. He goes there.

And then He does the thing nobody else can. He puts peace in your soul — not by removing the noise, but by anchoring you underneath it.

Quiet on the inside, even when it's loud out there. Can we pray for you? Text PRAY →
03 · Go in purpose

Purpose

You were made for something eternal.

Everyone is. Not everyone knows it. Most of what fills a life — the job, the house, the calendar — is real but temporary. It won't survive you.

But you were built to make something that lasts. To pour yourself into people and places where the results outlive your name on a building.

At SOJO you don't sit and watch that happen. You get sent — into your street, this city, the world. That's why we're at the parade and the tent and the tree lighting. A community with a cause.

Your one life was meant to leave a mark. Got a question about Jesus? Ask me →
