## Tier 1 — These feed everything else. Do these first.

**1. Google Business Profile** — business.google.com
This is the big one: it powers Google Search, Google Maps, and the map card people see when they search "SOJO Church." PC has the login. Update the address (Google may mail a verification postcard to the new suite — watch for it), and while you're in there update photos to the Kettle Room and confirm hours say Sundays 9 & 11am.

**2. Apple Maps** — register.apple.com/placesonmaps (Apple Business Connect, free)
Powers every iPhone's Maps app and Siri. If SOJO has never claimed its Apple listing, claim it with the church's Apple ID and correct the address. If it's unclaimed, anyone typing "SOJO Church" into an iPhone gets sent to Union Street.

**3. Facebook page** — [facebook.com/wearesojo.church](https://www.facebook.com/wearesojo.church/)
Edit the About section: address, and scan the page bio/intro text for "848 Union." Also pin a post about the move — Facebook's map checkin location updates from the About address.

**4. Instagram bio** — check the profile bio and any "location" line or linktree-style link for the old address.

**5. Bing Places** — bingplaces.com
Feeds Bing, and Bing feeds ChatGPT's answers. Easiest path: sign in and use "Import from Google Business Profile" AFTER #1 is done, and it copies everything over.
